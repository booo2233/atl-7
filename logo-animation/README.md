# De Paul Public School — kinetic logo reveal

A 15-second kinetic motion-graphics reveal of the school crest,
1920×1080 at 60 fps with a punchy, minimal soundtrack. Every frame and
every sound is generated by code.

**Output:** `output/de-paul-kinetic_1920x1080_60fps.mp4`

The look is flat brand colour (navy, crest blue, red, laurel green, gold, warm
paper), bold Inter Tight type with Instrument Serif Italic accents, hard
wipes, and real motion blur. The motion blur comes from averaging eight
sub-frames per frame, a 180° shutter. There are no glows or lens flares.
The cuts sit on a 120 BPM grid, one beat every 0.5 s.

## The sequence

| time (s) | what happens |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0 | A white line slices the navy frame and opens like a slit; **FOR** is inside. |
| 1.0–1.5 | Hard cut: **GOD** slams in. A red serif **&** spins in, and a red panel wipes across it. |
| 1.5–3.1 | **COUNTRY** tightens its tracking. Each letter then turns into a star and arcs up into the ring, landing on sixteenth notes. The ring draws itself and a line-art globe starts to spin. |
| 3.4–4.7 | A green wipe comes up from below and the laurel branches sprout, a pair of leaves on each sixteenth. |
| 4.7–5.5 | The white emblem collapses. A two-colour iris wipes to paper, repainting it in crest colours. It then hands over to the real crest artwork: the stars, globe and laurels. |
| 5.4–8.0 | The camera pulls back as the shield outline draws and fills. The book opens, the roundels draw, and the torch, rings and lamp pop in. The ribbon wipes on with its lettering, and the banner slams down with the motto. RAJAMUDY, the IDUKKI pill and the flourishes finish it. |
| 9.0–15 | The crest slides left into a lockup. **DE PAUL / PUBLIC / SCHOOL** rises from masks, followed by a red rule, "Rajamudy · Idukki" and "For God & Country". It then holds with a slow push-in. |

## How it works

- **`tools/extract_layers.py`** cuts the crest (`assets/source/logo.webp`)
  into 66 layers in `assets/layers/`, with a `manifest.json` of their
  positions, pivots and fitted circles.
- **`tools/extract_paths.py`** traces the shield border's centre line into
  `paths.json`, so the outline can draw itself.
- **`src/kinetic.js`** is the animation, in Canvas 2D. Every frame is a pure
  function of time. The line-art emblem (globe, ring, stars and laurels) is
  drawn as vectors. The crest is the extracted layers, animated with eased
  transforms and hard clip wipes.
- **`tools/sound.py`** synthesises the soundtrack with numpy from the
  animation's event table. The sounds are kick-style slams, band-passed noise
  swishes, dry pitched plucks for the stars, ticks for the leaves and
  letters, a sub drop and a final low hit.
- **`tools/render.mjs`** drives headless Chromium in parallel workers. Each
  worker reads back raw frames and pipes them to ffmpeg (H.264); the chunks
  are then joined and the soundtrack muxed in. A full render takes about two
  minutes on 4 CPU cores.

```
node tools/render.mjs --stills 1,2.5,5.2,9.8     # quick stills -> output/stills
node tools/render.mjs --mute --out output/video_only.mp4
python3 tools/sound.py                           # output/soundtrack.wav from output/events.json
node tools/render.mjs                            # full video with the soundtrack
```

Open `index.html` through any static server to play the reveal live, with
play/pause and a scrubber. Live playback runs without motion blur.

Fonts: Inter Tight and Instrument Serif, SIL Open Font License
(`assets/fonts/`). The first, glow-heavy version is still available as
`v1.html` / `src/logo.js` (`--page v1.html`).
